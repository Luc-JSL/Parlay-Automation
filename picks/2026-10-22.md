## PrizePicks card

**How to use this:** PrizePicks has only player More/Less picks. Each pick needs about
**54–59% to break even** (`python3 src/prizepicks.py breakeven`). A prop priced at -115 at a sportsbook
is only about 51–52% fair after removing the book's margin, so it's **not** a play on PrizePicks at the same line.
The edge comes from a PrizePicks line that is *softer than the sportsbook line*. Every pick below names
the line where it becomes a play. **If the PrizePicks line is not at or better than the threshold, skip it.**

| # | Player / stat | Pick | Sportsbook line (fair %) | Play on PrizePicks if line is | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drake Maye – Pass TDs | **More** | O1.5 at -116/-122 (~52%) | **1.5 only as a Goblin, or 0.5** | 10 TD / 1 INT over his last 3 games. Bears pass D is weak (Covers). |

| 3 | Drake Maye – Pass Yds | **Less** | U251.5 at -114 (~51%) | **≥ 263.5** | Two starting guards out, RT questionable. The Bears' pass rush could cut drives short. |
| 4 | Caleb Williams – Rush Yds | **More** | analyst lean 20+ | **≤ 16.5** | Scrambles more behind pressure. RB Monangai is out. |

How to build the entry:
- If 3+ of these clear their thresholds, play them as a **3- or 4-pick Power Play**.
- Picks 1 and 3 point in opposite directions (lots of TDs, few yards), so they partly hedge each other. Don't put both on one card unless both lines are clearly soft.
- If only 1–2 clear, don't force it. Add them to tomorrow's slate instead of filling the card with weak picks.

    How picks are rated

    • Market: each sportsbook's price is de-vigged, then moved to the PrizePicks line with a fitted stat distribution. The median across books is used. One point is taken off when the books sit at a different line, and half a point more when only two books post it.
    • History: how often the player beat this exact line in his last 20 games and last season, each pulled toward 50% so small samples don't dominate.
    • Estimate: 75% market, 25% history, plus any adjustment from injury, role or matchup research. Play is 58% or higher, lean is 55.5% or higher.
    • Rejected: the line sits outside PrizePicks' own goblin and demon ladder (usually a mislabeled line), the game logs disagree with the market, or research found a role or injury problem.
    • A slip clears the bar when its expected return is at least 1.05x and every pick beats the entry's break-even by 1.5 points. Add best only unlocks for a 2- or 3-pick slip that clears it, or a bigger one returning at least 1.15x.
    • Before you enter: confirm each line in the app is a standard line (More and Less both offered) at the same number or better, and check the payout shown there.

    Risk 1–3% of your bankroll per entry, never chase losses, and treat this as entertainment, not income. A well-reasoned pick still loses often; only long-run results matter.
